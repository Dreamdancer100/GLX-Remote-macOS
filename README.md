## 📖 About GLX Remote macOS

### Help for your Mac, made easy

GLX Remote for macOS is the Mac side of GLX Remote. Your helper sits at a Windows PC with GLX Remote and can connect to your iMac or MacBook as if sitting right in front of it. You only give them your GLX ID and password, both shown large in the window.

### You decide

Every connection has to be allowed on the Mac first. When someone asks, their name appears with "Allow" and "Deny"; without an answer the request is denied automatically after 60 seconds. You can disconnect at any time, create a new password with one click or switch connections off completely. After several wrong passwords GLX Remote blocks further attempts for a few minutes.

### See and control

The helper sees your screen live and uses mouse and keyboard. With several screens they can switch between them. The keyboard is set up for German keyboards on both sides: Ctrl on the PC becomes Command on the Mac, so copy and paste work as usual.

### Chat, clipboard and sound

In the built-in chat you write to each other during the session. Copied text can travel between PC and Mac via the clipboard. When the helper speaks, you hear them on your Mac, and you switch your microphone on only at the press of a button.

### Files in both directions

The helper can send you files and fetch files from your Mac. They see Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures and connected drives; hidden files stay hidden. Incoming files land in the Downloads folder under "GLX Remote".

### Home network and internet

On the same network PC and Mac connect directly; on the road the connection runs via the GLX server. In both cases everything is end-to-end encrypted. A display shows the helper how fast the connection currently is.

### Always reachable

If you close the window, GLX Remote stays in the menu bar and remains reachable for your helper. Your GLX ID on the Mac always stays the same.

### First start on the Mac

GLX Remote comes directly from GLX Tools and not from the App Store, so macOS shows a warning the first time you open it. Right-click GLX Remote once and choose "Open", or allow the app in System Settings under "Privacy & Security" with "Open Anyway". After that GLX Remote starts normally.

### Two permissions, only once

macOS protects the screen and input especially well, so GLX Remote needs two permissions once. The "Grant" button in the program takes you straight to the right place in System Settings under "Privacy & Security".

Screen Recording: so your helper can see your screen. Switch GLX Remote on here. Without this permission the Mac accepts no connection.

Accessibility: so your helper can use mouse and keyboard. Switch GLX Remote on here too.

If macOS asks whether to restart GLX Remote, choose "Quit & Reopen". Both permissions survive updates, so you only grant them once.

### Beautiful and simple

12 designs, German and English, tooltips on every button. A session log helps with questions.

## ✨ Features

- 🚀 **One app for all devices:** Control Windows PCs, Macs, Android phones and tablets and Android TV from your Mac.
- 🧩 **Matching view per device:** GLX Remote detects the device type and opens the right view, with an on-screen remote for TVs.
- ⚙️ **Favourites with own names:** Every device has a fixed GLX ID; save it as "Mum's laptop" and connect with one click.
- 🔒 **Local and internet:** Devices in your network are found automatically; elsewhere the GLX relay server connects you.
- 📂 **Three quality levels:** Choose the image quality that fits the internet connection.
- ⚡ **Mac keys for Windows:** Mouse and keyboard with translation of Mac keys for Windows PCs.
- 🎨 **Chat, sound and microphone:** Talk and write with the other side during the session.
- 🌍 **Files and clipboard:** Transfer files in both directions with a file explorer and share the clipboard.
- 🛠️ **End-to-end encrypted:** ECDH and AES-256; the relay only forwards data and cannot read it. The other side decides whether to accept.
- 📊 **Real Mac style:** Native app for Intel and Apple silicon, window buttons top left, 12 designs, German and English, built-in help.
